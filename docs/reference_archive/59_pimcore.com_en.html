PIMCORE DOMAINS

Product Information Management (PIM) – Product Information

The Problem: Product attributes live in spreadsheets and ERP exports — scattered across teams, incomplete by default, with no completeness tracking and no single place to govern them.

Pimcore Capabilities

  • Data Quality & Completeness Scoring
  • Data Modelling (45+ field types, variant inheritance)
  • Multichannel Publishing

Impact & Benefits

  • Faster time-to-market through automated validation and publish workflows
  • Fewer returns and support requests driven by accurate, complete product data
  • One source of truth across all channels, markets, and teams

Master Data Management (MDM) – Master Data

The Problem: Master data lives in silos: ERP says one thing, PIM says another. Dependencies between entities — categories, manufacturers, accessories — are invisible, untracked, and never resolved.

Pimcore Capabilities

  • Golden Record Management
  • Conflict Resolution
  • Data Governance & Lineage

Impact & Benefits

  • Consistent master data across ERP, PIM, and commerce — no more manual reconciliation
  • Full auditability: every field change traceable to its source and approver
  • Reduced integration complexity as downstream systems always consume one clean record

Digital Asset Management (DAM) – Digital Assets

The Problem: Assets live in shared drives and inboxes — no approval workflow, no usage rights tracking, and no way to know which product a file is actually attached to.

Pimcore Capabilities

  • Asset Metadata & Rights Management
  • Workflow & Approval
  • Product Linkage

Impact & Benefits

  • Eliminated licence risk through centrally tracked rights and expiry dates
  • Faster campaign delivery with approved, product-linked assets always ready to use
  • No more duplicate files or lost originals — one versioned repository for the entire brand

Customer Data Platform (CDP) – Customer Data

The Problem: Customer data sits in separate systems — CRM holds contact details, the shop holds orders, analytics holds behaviour. No system sees the full picture, so no one can act on it.

Pimcore Capabilities

  • Unified Customer Profile
  • Segmentation & Audience Builder
  • Activity Timeline

Impact & Benefits

  • Higher campaign relevance through segments built on real, unified behaviour
  • Shorter sales cycles as teams act on complete profiles rather than fragmented data
  • GDPR-compliant data governance with consent and source tracked per profile

Digital Experience Platform (DXP) – Digital Experience

The Problem: Each market maintains its own page tree, its own translations, and its own publish workflow. The same content exists in dozens of versions — inconsistent, outdated, impossible to govern.

Pimcore Capabilities

  • Multisite & Multilingual Management
  • Personalisation & Targeting
  • Workflow & Approval

Impact & Benefits

  • Brand consistency across all markets enforced from a single content layer
  • Faster localisation with shared base content and market-specific overrides only where needed
  • Higher conversion through audience-targeted content without duplicating page trees

CHANNELS

Website & Web Experience (Website) – 40 regional sites

The Problem: Every regional site is its own build: content copied by hand, layouts drifting apart, campaigns going live weeks late. The website reflects whatever data happened to be pasted in, not the single source of truth.

Pimcore Capabilities

  • Headless & Hybrid Delivery via APIs
  • Multisite, Multilingual Page Management
  • Personalisation & Audience Targeting

Impact & Benefits

  • One content and product layer feeds 40+ regional sites — publish once, appear everywhere
  • Marketing ships campaigns and landing pages without developer involvement
  • Consistent, on-brand experiences that always reflect current product and asset data

Commerce & Ordering (Commerce) – B2C, B2B, Headless

The Problem: Catalogue, pricing, and stock live in different systems than the storefront. B2C, B2B, and headless channels each get a slightly different version of the truth, and every integration is bespoke and brittle.

Pimcore Capabilities

  • Unified B2C, B2B & Headless Commerce
  • Pricing, Catalogue & Order Management
  • Checkout & Fulfilment Workflows

Impact & Benefits

  • A single catalogue and pricing engine serving every commerce channel
  • Faster launch of new markets and B2B portals on shared product data
  • Fewer order errors as stock, price, and product data stay in sync end-to-end

Dealer & Partner Portals (Portals) – Dealer & partner

The Problem: Dealers and partners work from PDFs, email threads, and out-of-date exports. There is no self-service access to current product data, pricing, or documents — every request lands on an internal team.

Pimcore Capabilities

  • Dealer & Partner Self-Service Portals
  • Role-Based Access to Products, Pricing & Docs
  • Personalised Catalogues & Order Entry

Impact & Benefits

  • Partners pull live product data, assets, and pricing themselves — no manual fulfilment
  • Role-based views show each partner exactly what they are entitled to see
  • Reduced internal workload and faster partner onboarding on shared, governed data

AI AGENT BUILT FOR YOUR NEEDS

Enrich & Complete Data (Enrich) – Auto-complete attrs

The Problem: Specialists enrich products by hand — cross-referencing supplier datasheets, brand guidelines, and classification rules. A team of five clears maybe 200 products a week, and completeness gaps still slip through to the channels.

Pimcore Capabilities

  • Enrichment agents that read the data model, required attributes & classification rules
  • Pulls & reconciles data from connected systems via governed MCP tools
  • Output validated against completeness rules before it enters the workflow

Impact & Benefits

  • First 80% of enrichment automated — the same team moves toward 2,000 products a week
  • Agents act within the initiating user's permissions, so governance is enforced from day one
  • Same quality gates, approval workflows, and audit trails as human-curated data

Translate Across Markets (Translate) – 23 languages

The Problem: Localisation runs in batches: a quarterly export to an agency, returned weeks later, manually re-imported and reviewed. Markets wait, and terminology drifts between languages and channels.

Pimcore Capabilities

  • Translation agents triggered the moment source content is approved
  • Applies market-specific terminology, regulatory wording & brand voice per locale
  • Translations enter the standard review workflow immediately, not as a separate track

Impact & Benefits

  • Turnaround drops from weeks to hours across all target markets at once
  • Continuous localisation instead of quarterly batches — markets stay in sync
  • Source-language content is protected: a translation agent cannot alter the original

Generate Content at Scale (Generate) – Copy & PDPs

The Problem: Channel output is configured by hand — a feed for the marketplace, a different format for the website, another export for print. Every channel gets the same content, just reformatted, with no real adaptation.

Pimcore Capabilities

  • Content agents generating channel-optimised copy from the governed Data Spine
  • SEO-oriented for marketplaces, technical for B2B, lifestyle for the consumer site
  • Bound by the same brand and quality rules as a human copywriter

Impact & Benefits

  • One governed source produces genuinely channel-fit content — not reformatted duplicates
  • Output is validated and approved before it reaches any channel
  • Scales content production without scaling the editorial headcount

Predict Demand & Score Leads (Predict) – Demand & lead score

The Problem: Demand signals and lead data sit in separate systems, reviewed after the fact. Quality issues — a wrong price, a missing image, an incomplete record — surface only when someone notices, usually downstream.

Pimcore Capabilities

  • Monitoring agents continuously scanning the spine for anomalies & gaps
  • Demand and lead scoring grounded in unified, governed profiles
  • Issues flagged and workflows triggered before content reaches a channel

Impact & Benefits

  • Quality becomes proactive: problems caught before they hit a customer touchpoint
  • Predictions run on trusted, semantically-rich data — not a flat attribute dump
  • Escalation triggers route low-confidence or high-value cases to a human automatically

ANY SYSTEMS

Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) – Cost, BOM, weight

The Problem: The ERP is authoritative for cost, finance, and stock — but that data is locked inside it. Getting it into product, commerce, and marketing workflows means brittle point-to-point exports that break on every schema change.

Pimcore Capabilities

  • Datahub connector ingesting cost, BOM, weight & stock from the ERP
  • Field-level mapping with configurable precedence and conflict rules
  • Live, event-driven sync — data is delivered, not copied

Impact & Benefits

  • The ERP stays the system of record; the spine resolves conflicts and adds context
  • No more batch exports drifting out of date — downstream always sees current data
  • One governed integration instead of a separate pipeline per consuming system

Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) – Engineering specs

The Problem: Engineering specifications, bills of materials, and CAD metadata live in the PLM, disconnected from how products are actually sold and marketed. Specs are re-keyed by hand, and updates never reach the channels.

Pimcore Capabilities

  • Ingestion of engineering specs, BOM & lifecycle data from the PLM
  • Mapping of technical attributes into the governed product model
  • Relationships preserved: variants, components & classification context

Impact & Benefits

  • Engineering truth flows into product data without manual re-entry
  • Technical specs stay accurate across B2B portals, datasheets, and the website
  • Full lineage from published spec back to its authoritative PLM source

Supplier Data Integration (Suppliers) – Parts & compliance

The Problem: Supplier data arrives as inconsistent spreadsheets and emails — parts, compliance documents, and availability in dozens of formats, with no validation and no way to trust what is current.

Pimcore Capabilities

  • Connectors for supplier parts, compliance & availability data
  • Validation and deduplication on ingest, against the governed data model
  • Conflict resolution when suppliers disagree with internal records

Impact & Benefits

  • Supplier data is reconciled and quality-checked before it reaches any product record
  • Compliance documents tracked with provenance — audit-ready by design
  • Faster supplier onboarding on a structured, governed intake instead of manual cleanup
