WordPress advantage

Your site, your rules

  • Open platform, not a locked box

    Hosted gallery platforms give you their features and nothing more. Sunshine runs on WordPress, so you can customize everything: add your own CSS, modify templates, or extend functionality with hooks and filters.

  • AI makes customization easy

    With the help of AI, you don't need to be a developer to add small, specific features that make Sunshine work exactly the way you want for your workflow. That's the power of an open WordPress plugin, and it's actively encouraged. A closed hosted platform can't offer that.

  • No switching costs

    Your photos, your orders, your customer data — it all lives in your WordPress database. You're never locked into a platform that could raise prices, change terms, or shut down.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is Sunshine Photo Cart Free?
    The core Sunshine Photo Cart plugin is free and has all the necessary features needed to create galleries, sell to clients, and even use PayPal, Square*, and Stripe* payment gateways. Add-ons are available to enhance your photo sales which you can purchase individually or as part of a plan.
  • Do I have to use WordPress?
    Yes, Sunshine Photo Cart is a WordPress plugin and will only work on WordPress websites. It will not work on any other website platform like Wix, Squarespace, etc.
  • Do I have to purchase add-ons or a plan to use Sunshine?
    No! Add-ons are entirely optional but do help to dramatically extend the functionality of Sunshine Photo Cart. If you just want to download the free Sunshine Photo Cart core plugin, click here.
  • What about file storage?
    Photographers take lots of photos and use a lot of file storage. The Cloud Storage add-on lets you offload your gallery images to the provider of your choice — including Amazon S3, Backblaze B2, Bunny.net, Cloudflare R2, and more. Your upload workflow stays the same, and images are served directly from the cloud for fast loading.
  • Can I purchase add-ons individually?
    Yes, each add-on may be purchased individually. However, you will save significantly if you purchase the Pro bundle which includes every add-on for a heavily discounted price.
  • How many sites can I use a license on?
    Plans and individual add-ons include a single site license by default. Multi-site licenses for 5 or 15 sites are also available.
  • What happens if I do not renew my license?
    License keys are subscription-based and will automatically renew every year. If you decide to cancel, you will have access to use the add-ons until your license expires. Expired add-ons will no longer work but your data will remain in case you renew your license later.
  • Can I request a refund?
    You are more than welcome to request a refund within 14 days of your purchase for any reason. Renewal payments are non-refundable. View full terms and conditions.
